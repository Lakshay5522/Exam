FormFill

Turn any Google Form link into an auto-filled link on this server.

preview answers before opening
Easiest way: paste the whole Google Form URL right after the server address —
http:///https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/.../viewform
The server fills it in the background and redirects straight to the filled form — no loading page. Add a keyword to fill as a specific person from profiles.json: http://server/docs.lakshay.google.com/forms/... or /lakshay/https://docs....
Also available: /f/, ?preview=1 (review answers first), ?p=name.
